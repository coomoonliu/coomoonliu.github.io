Process

How the platform works

Produce personal digital assets, publish them in your space, trade on the shared market — settlement stays evidence-based.

A. Produce → space → trade (user value)

Open the mobile terminal Create tab. Mint a platform-ledger digital product or utility token, publish it into your personal metaverse space, then list it on the shared marketplace. Listing is an offer only — Settled is never invented here.

Open Create

B1. Receive settlement instructions (optional parallel)

You receive a payment email with wire details (and optional crypto rail). The link opens this detached public payment guide with your order ID.

B2. Transfer via HSBC wire

Send USD to the published beneficiary account. Include the order ID in the remittance memo when your bank allows it.

B3. Reply with evidence

Reply with remittance / SWIFT reference (or crypto tx id). The local control plane ingests evidence - this public host does not confirm Settled balances.

B4. Recognition

Settled capital is recognized only after verified remittance or crypto evidence matches the order. Public pages never invent Settled amounts.
