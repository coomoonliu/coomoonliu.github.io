Privacy & data

How public and control data are separated

Public surface

This host publishes sealed envelopes and contact channels only. It does not expose local IP addresses, operator tunnels, machine identifiers, or plaintext wire/crypto details.

Floating encrypted storage

Sensitive operational payloads are AES-GCM encrypted, XOR-sharded, and stored as opaque data shards on independent external endpoints. Nonce, tag, and DEK shards remain in the local controlled key domain. External multi-points cannot independently read or synthesize content.

Capital recognition

Settled balances are recognized only after verified remittance or crypto evidence is ingested by the control plane. Public pages never invent capital.
