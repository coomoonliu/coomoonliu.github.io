Frequently asked questions

Why does the payment link go to github.io?

The public site is a detached identity so counterparties are not pointed at a LAN IP or ephemeral tunnel that could fingerprint the control machine.

Is the wire information complete without the link?

Yes. Payment emails embed HSBC wire details. The public guide repeats the same beneficiary route and attaches your order ID.

Where is crypto deposit information?

When issued, crypto rails are delivered in email. They are intentionally not mirrored on the static public host.

When is capital Settled?

Only after verified bank remittance or crypto evidence is ingested by the control plane. This website does not create Settled balances.

Public presence surface only. Local control plane is not exposed on this host. Settled capital is recognized only after verified remittance / crypto evidence - never invented.
